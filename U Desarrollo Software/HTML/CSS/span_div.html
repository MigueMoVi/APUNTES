Solution: To begin, suppose that there are n players, with each player initially having 1 unit. Consider player i. Each stage she plays will be equally likely to result in her either winning or losing 1 unit, with the results from each stage being independent. In addition, she will continue to play stages until her fortune becomes either 0 or n. Because this is the same for all players, it follows that each player has the same chance of being the victor. 

	Consequently, each player has player probability 1/n of being the victor. Now, suppose these n players are divided into r teams,
